# Mod sound effects

The finder and builder suggest mods using the effects below, on the same scale as the sound estimate (pitch and volume run 0 to 100). The catalog refresh reads each mod's type from its listing name (`modOf` and `MOD_TYPES` in `scripts/catalog-lib.mjs`). Each effect is the usual community result for that type of mod. None of them are measurements of a particular product.

| Type | Pitch | Volume | What changes | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Case foam | −5 | −8 | Less hollow echo and case ping, fuller and deeper | [Glorious](https://www.gloriousgaming.com/blogs/guides-resources/what-is-the-purpose-of-keyboard-foam), [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2270716/what-foam-does-mechanical-keyboard/), [Evetech](https://evezone.evetech.co.za/quick-bytes/keyboard-sound-mods-explained-foam-tape-pe-sheets) |
| Plate foam (plate to PCB) | −3 | −4 | Softens the plate's ring and high overtones, less PCB flex | [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2270716/what-foam-does-mechanical-keyboard/), [KeebTalk comparison](https://www.keebtalk.com/t/plate-foam-with-and-without-comparison-mkultra-click-plate-foam/6487) |
| PE switch pads | +5 | −1 | Poppy, marbly bottom-out, brighter attack | [Switch and Click](https://switchandclick.com/pe-foam-mod/), [Evetech](https://evezone.evetech.co.za/quick-bytes/keyboard-sound-mods-explained-foam-tape-pe-sheets) |
| Tape mod | −5 | −4 | Cuts high frequencies, a fuller sound | [Dygma](https://dygma.com/blogs/product-development/top-10-mods-to-improve-the-sound-of-your-keyboard), [Evetech](https://evezone.evetech.co.za/quick-bytes/keyboard-sound-mods-explained-foam-tape-pe-sheets) |
| O-rings | −2 | −6 | Much quieter (sources cite about 5 to 10 dB), a lower thud, and a shorter, softer landing | [Wooting](https://wooting.io/post/sound-dampen-your-mechanical-keyboard-noises/), [Dygma](https://dygma.com/blogs/product-development/top-10-mods-to-improve-the-sound-of-your-keyboard) |
| Switch films | −1 | −1 | Tighter housings, less wobble and rattle, a modest effect | [Unikeyboards](https://unikeyboards.com/blogs/weekeebpedia/what-are-keyboard-switch-films) |
| Switch lube (205g0, 3203/3204) | −3 | −3 | Smoother, less scratch, deeper and cleaner | [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/uk/how-to/lube-switches-mechanical-keyboard), [KeebsForAll 205g0](https://keebsforall.com/products/krytox-205-g0-switch-lubricant) |
| Spring oil (GPL 105/106) | 0 | −1 | Removes spring ping (vendor claim only) | [Thocc Exchange](https://thoccexchange.com/products/krytox-gpl-105-oil-lubricant) |
| Stabilizer lube (XHT-BDZ, stabilizer grease) | 0 | −1 | Stops stabilizer rattle on the long keys | [KeebsForAll XHT-BDZ](https://keebsforall.com/products/krytox-xht-bdz-lubricant) |

Spacebar and stabilizer foam is not treated as a sound mod, because no source describing its effect was found.

A mod that names a board is only suggested for that board. Foam and pads that name only a layout come with a "check it fits" note. Lube, films and O-rings fit any board.
