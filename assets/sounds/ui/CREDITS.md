# Interface sound credits

The site's interface sounds are single key presses cut from these Freesound recordings, each released under the license shown.

- `key-1.wav`: Picking a quiz answer (one of three presses, chosen at random). From "Single Key Presses on Mechanical Keyboard" by Xemptful, https://freesound.org/s/660387/ (CC0 1.0), cut at 1.470 s, filtered and level-matched.
- `key-2.wav`: Picking a quiz answer. From "Single Key Presses on Mechanical Keyboard" by Xemptful, https://freesound.org/s/660387/ (CC0 1.0), cut at 2.972 s, filtered and level-matched.
- `key-3.wav`: Picking a quiz answer. From "Single Key Presses on Mechanical Keyboard" by Xemptful, https://freesound.org/s/660387/ (CC0 1.0), cut at 4.056 s, filtered and level-matched.
- `confirm.wav`: Copying a share link. From "Single Key Presses on Mechanical Keyboard" by Xemptful, https://freesound.org/s/660387/ (CC0 1.0), cut at 1.837 s, filtered and level-matched.
- `settle.wav`: Opening a build (a larger key, like a spacebar). From "Single_Key_Stroke_Large_Key" by Capt.Jack, https://freesound.org/s/813210/ (CC0 1.0), cut at 0.084 s, filtered and level-matched.
