# Sound example credits

The quiz's sound examples are cut from these Freesound recordings. Each is released under the license shown.

- **Deep thock** (`thock.mp3`): "Typing on a rainy 75" by Techrul, https://freesound.org/s/815614/ (CC0 1.0). Wobkey Rainy75. Trimmed to 10 s from 0.5 s and loudness-matched.
- **Creamy** (`creamy.mp3`): "Thocky Keyboard Typing" by aliyahb, https://freesound.org/s/869164/ (CC0 1.0). Mechanical keyboard with a soft, low-mid sound. Trimmed to 10 s from 1.75 s and loudness-matched.
- **Crisp clack** (`clack.mp3`): "Typing On Keyboard (cherry mx browns)" by Seth_Makes_Sounds, https://freesound.org/s/618064/ (CC0 1.0). Cherry MX Brown switches, condenser microphone. Trimmed to 10 s from 31 s and loudness-matched.
- **Quiet** (`quiet.mp3`): "Typing Fast_A01.wav" by bonesawmgraw, https://freesound.org/s/572978/ (CC0 1.0). Cherry MX Silent switches. Trimmed to 10 s from 1.75 s and loudness-matched.
